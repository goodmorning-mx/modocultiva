# Activity Bank: editorial research and effort model

Reviewed 26 October 2026. The bank contains 28 short verified excerpts, curated into ten distinct quote/topic pairs for each of the 12 modes. Relevant excerpts may recur across different modes and levels. Each deck has ten different quotations. The practical suggestions are authored for Modo Cultiva; they are not statements or endorsements by the quoted people.

Original wording is retained in English. Spanish translations are editorial and identified as such. Other interface languages identify the English original explicitly; the activity topics, instructions, navigation and resource labels are localized into all 11 supported languages. Sources expand on request and link to the original document. Short passages are presented as excerpts; no claim is made that these authors invented every proverb they quoted. In particular Franklin's text itself attributes sayings to Poor Richard, his literary persona.

Nobel's live page rejected direct text fetching (403); its indexed primary-source print page supplied the two checked excerpts. The remaining documents were directly accessible. Popular attributions without a verifiable original were excluded. The frequently quoted 'Stay hungry, stay foolish' was not attributed to Jobs as an original saying.

## Mapping and effort

The catalog explicitly pairs ten subjects with citations per mode: bodily awareness and rest; inquiry and sources; feelings and interpretations; values and consistency; meaning and contemplation; partnership; family care; friendship and community; spending awareness and resource planning; work and skills; restorative play; goals and legacy. The association is an editorial interpretation, not scientific evidence about a quotation.

Every one of the ten choices uses the **selected journey level** for effort. Slide position never increases difficulty.

| Level | Application | Time / cadence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | Notice a signal, without requiring change | 2 minutes once |
| 1 | Identify one need and a possible response | 3 minutes once |
| 2 | Choose a small, free first step | 5 minutes once |
| 3 | Try a short practice | 10 minutes twice in a week |
| 4 | Establish consistency | 15 minutes three times in a week |
| 5 | Maintain a working routine, remove friction | 15 minutes once in a week |
| 6 | Set a goal and allocate available resources | 30 minutes weekly over four weeks |
| 7 | Record results and adjust a variable | 45 minutes weekly over four weeks |
| 8 | Improve the system, try optional support | 45 minutes, compare results |
| 9 | Document and share experience, with consent | Review after a month |

These are bounded starting suggestions, not validated clinical, financial or performance prescriptions. Advanced activities allocate resources within the person's means; spending money is not compulsory. Level 9 remains the existing vision-board journey in the UI; the catalog has a complete level-9 definition for future consumers. Existing Global Ideas content is kept separate from this catalog.

Using an activity saves the selected topic and the concrete instruction (including duration and cadence), rather than saving the quotation or the author's name as an activity. No storage format or migration changes are introduced.
